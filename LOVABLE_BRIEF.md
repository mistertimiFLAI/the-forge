# The Forge website brief

Build a premium, cinematic one-page website for a Christian formation and career-pathway organisation called **The Forge**.

## Brand and purpose

The public brand is only **The Forge**. Do not mention TwC, TwC Group or any parent organisation anywhere on the site.

The Forge equips young adults and early-career professionals to enter and lead in the marketplace with Christian character, conviction and capability.

The tone is weighty, aspirational, modern, biblical and grounded. This is not a generic church or youth charity website. It should feel like formation under heat: strength, purpose, discipline, practical confidence and meaningful work.

## Visual direction

- Dark charcoal / blackened-steel foundation.
- Warm ember orange as the accent colour, around `#9e3913` or `#ff6822`.
- Off-white / parchment text and surfaces.
- Use **Poppins** as the primary typeface.
- Use **Playfair Display**, italic, weight 600 for the belief statement and the word “Kingdom” in the hero.
- Use large editorial typography, spacious layouts and clean, confident composition.
- Use cinematic imagery of a real forge, anvil and glowing metal as the full-screen hero background.
- Use subtle texture, warmth and restrained motion only.
- Do not use tech grids, floating dots, radial-line patterns, neon, fantasy-medieval styling or generic church stock imagery.
- The logo should be a clean, bold anvil silhouette with one subtle hot ember seam. It should feel wearable on a hoodie and work in one colour. No crosses, shields, monograms or extra symbols.

## Required page structure and copy

### 1. Full-screen hero

Header: anvil mark + “The Forge”

Navigation links: “Our pathways” and “Get involved”

Hero heading, exactly:

> Forged for the Kingdom.  
> Built for the marketplace.

Give the heading slightly expanded letter spacing, not tight.

Supporting copy, semi-bold:

> The Forge equips young adults and early-career professionals to enter and lead in the marketplace with Christian character, conviction and capability.

CTAs:

- Find your next step
- Explore the pathways

Include Proverbs 27:17 subtly at the bottom:

> “As iron sharpens iron, so one person sharpens another.”  
> Proverbs 27:17

### 2. What we believe

Use a generous, simple layout with three full-size lines. All three lines must use **Playfair Display, italic, weight 600** and remain the same font size.

> Living for Christ is not confined to Sundays or private moments.  
> Together, we sharpen one another to lead, work and serve in the marketplace with the character and grace of Christ.  
> Christ first. Always.

Styling requirements:

- The words **“lead, work and serve”** use the ember accent colour.
- **“Christ first. Always.”** uses the ember accent colour.
- Add a small eyebrow label: “What we believe”.
- Use a cream / parchment section background: `#f4f0e8`.

### 3. Pathways

Use three equal, clean cards. Card headings use the ember accent colour.

**01 / Participate**  
**Step forward.**

- Coaching and mentoring
- Faith for everyday work
- Practical pathways into opportunity

**02 / Mentor**  
**Sharpen another.**

- Share your experience
- Build confidence and direction
- Walk alongside someone

**03 / Partner**  
**Open a door.**

- Open routes into work
- Support apprenticeships and placements
- Create meaningful opportunity

### 4. Closing CTA

Heading:

> There is more in you.

Copy:

> Whether you are taking your first step, ready to mentor, or can open a door for someone else, The Forge is a place to begin.

Button: **Register your interest**

For now, make the CTA open a polished interest form/modal with fields for name, email and “I want to…” with options: Step forward, Mentor, Partner.

Do not invent an email address, phone number, address, testimonials, statistics, partners, programme dates or impact claims.

### 5. Footer

> The Forge · Forming people for life, work and leadership

## Technical and UX requirements

- Responsive and excellent on mobile.
- Smooth but restrained motion only: subtle ember glow, background image movement and small hover states.
- Accessible contrast, readable type and clear focus states.
- Build a polished production-ready landing page, not a wireframe.
